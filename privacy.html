GroWonder Privacy Policy

Device Info: Device ID, OS, browser type

Usage Data: Booking history, milestone tracking, user actions

2. How We Use Your Information

Enable bookings and progress tracking.

Personalize your experience.

Communicate updates and expert content.

Improve platform performance and safety.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data.

We may share minimal necessary data with:

Service providers you book with.

Legal authorities, if required.

Secure cloud and infrastructure vendors.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored using encrypted protocols on secure servers.

Only authorized personnel may access it.

You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not collect personal data from children under 18 directly.

All accounts must be created and managed by a parent or guardian.

6. Third-Party Services

We may integrate third-party analytics, payment, or communication tools.

Each may have its own privacy policy.
